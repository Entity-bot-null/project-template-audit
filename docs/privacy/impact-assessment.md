# Data protection impact assessment: Audit Hub

Versión en español: [impact-assessment.es.md](impact-assessment.es.md).

> Template, not legal advice. Review by counsel before use. Last checked against the law: 2026-10-17.

A data protection impact assessment of the security audit log that a connected application keeps
in Audit Hub, pre-filled with what Audit Hub does. The controller completes it for its
application, with the operator's help, and signs it off.

## Is it required?

- **LFPDPPP: not for private parties.** The 2025 law gives no private responsable the duty to
  carry out an impact assessment; it only gives the regulator the power to prepare impact studies
  (LFPDPPP art. 39 fr. IX). The public-sector law's assessment (LGPDPPSO 2025, arts. 68–73) does
  not apply to private parties.
- **Recommended anyway.** The 2011 Reglamento requires a risk analysis as part of the security
  measures (art. 61 fr. III), a procedure to address the risks of new products and technologies
  (art. 48 fr. V), and updated measures after substantial changes (art. 62 fr. II). Its status
  under the 2025 law is **not confirmed (needs legal review)**; this assessment serves either
  way.
- **GDPR, for users in the EU:** an assessment is required where a processing is likely to result
  in a high risk (GDPR art. 35). A security log of every sign-in and device may count as
  systematic monitoring. **Needs legal review:** check the lists of the EDPB and of the competent
  national authority.

## 1. Systematic description

### Purposes

1. Protect the users' accounts and the application: detect unusual sign-ins, password guessing,
   session theft and misuse ([security.md](../security.md#detection-rules)).
2. Prevent fraud and account takeover, including asking the user "Was it you?"
   ([connection-guide.md](../audit/connection-guide.md#webhooks-was-it-you-and-the-accounts-activity)).
3. Accountability: a record of who did what and when that cannot be changed unnoticed
   ([security.md](../security.md#audit-log-integrity-retention-and-erasure)).
4. Legal compliance and answers to competent authorities.
5. With separate consent: the phone's location when a session starts, approximate or, if the
   person allows it, precise, for sign-in alerts
   ([connection-guide.md](../audit/connection-guide.md#location-optional)).

### How the data flow

1. The application's API records each event in its own database, in the transaction of the change
   it describes, with the request's IP, user agent and client hints; emails become keyed
   pseudonyms, phones their last four digits and sensitive fields `[REDACTED]` before they leave
   ([connection-guide.md](../audit/connection-guide.md#what-the-connector-does)).
2. The mobile app and the web app send their own events (a session that began or expired, a link
   refused, a blocked script) to the application's API, never to Audit Hub
   ([connection-guide.md](../audit/connection-guide.md#the-mobile-app),
   [connection-guide.md](../audit/connection-guide.md#the-web-app)).
3. The API forwards signed batches to Audit Hub every 2 seconds.
4. Audit Hub validates and stores them, refuses a phone that is not masked, keeps network and
   device data apart, seals each stream every minute and verifies everything daily
   ([security.md](../security.md#audit-log-integrity-retention-and-erasure)).
5. The rules read every event and raise alerts. Alerts go to the application by webhook and, when
   enabled, to administrators and auditors by email and to chat channels
   ([security.md](../security.md#detection-rules)).
6. People with an Audit Hub account read the events in the admin pages; every read is recorded
   ([security.md](../security.md#audit-hubs-own-log-exports-and-the-query-api)).
7. An administrator may ask the application to act on a user; the application acts and reports
   the outcome ([security.md](../security.md#webhooks-and-response-actions)).
8. Retention deletes the data daily when their period ends. An erasure deletes a user's personal
   data at once, or their events too, and blocks what remains for them; at the end of the
   service, the application's whole log is deleted and the application retired
   ([security.md](../security.md#audit-log-integrity-retention-and-erasure)).
